NutriSnap: AI Calorie Tracker

## Short description (max 80 chars)
Snap meals for on-device AI estimates of calories and macros.

## Full description (max 4000 chars)

**Snap it. Count it.**

NutriSnap is a privacy-first calorie tracker that runs entirely on your device. Point your camera at any meal and Pip — your on-device AI — identifies what's on your plate and estimates calories and macros in seconds. Scan photos are not uploaded for analysis. No NutriSnap account is required for tracking.

**🥭 AI-Powered Food Recognition**
Snap a photo of any meal and let on-device AI identify your food and estimate nutrition — instantly, and completely offline.

**🔒 On-Device Scanning**
After downloading the AI model, you can scan meals offline. The scan runs locally. Ads, Premium purchases, diagnostics, and optional reports use an internet connection. Review the privacy policy before sending public feedback.

**📊 Calorie & Macro Tracking**
Get calories, protein, carbs, and fat for every meal. Track daily rings for calories, protein, and hydration goals at a glance.

**📓 Meal Diary & History**
Browse past entries, spot trends over time, and log meals manually, by description, or by barcode scan — whatever's fastest for you.

**🔥 Streaks & Progress**
Stay motivated with streak tracking, daily challenges, badges, and weight trend charts.

**🎨 Fully Customizable**
Set your own calorie and macro goals, choose metric or imperial units, and pick light, dark, or system theme.

**🐾 Meet Pip**
Your friendly on-device AI companion cheers you on, reacts to your progress, and keeps things fun.

**Why On-Device AI?**
Most calorie trackers send your food photos to the cloud. NutriSnap’s food recognition runs locally instead. Meal records are stored on your device; see the privacy policy for Android backup and anything you choose to share.

**Free & Premium**
Core tracking, meal diary, and a limited number of free AI scans are completely free. NutriSnap Premium unlocks unlimited AI scans and removes ads, with monthly and yearly options.

**For General Wellness**
Nutrition estimates can be wrong. NutriSnap is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified health professional for medical advice.

**Requirements**
NutriSnap requires a device with 6 GB+ RAM to run the on-device AI model smoothly.
